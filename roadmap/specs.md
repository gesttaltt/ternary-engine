## Data & Integration Requirements
- Functional requirements demand extraction, comparison, and update of EBASE environments with multi-format exports (Excel, JSON, CSV, SQL) while staying within five minutes for 10,000 records, aligning with large-scale configuration deployments common in utility control centers (exemplars/agile/epic-exemplar.md:74, exemplars/agile/epic-exemplar.md:77, exemplars/agile/epic-exemplar.md:81).
- The Unit Conversion API spec positions this service as the authoritative conversion engine for the eBase energy ecosystem and enumerates downstream consumers such as market portals, billing systems, reporting tools, and data warehouses (exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:21, exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:23, exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:29).
- Non-functional requirements call for <200 ms single-conversion latency, <500 ms batch latency, and throughput above 10,000 conversions/sec, highlighting the scale at which market data is processed (exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:96, exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:102, exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:363).
- Long-running or asynchronous conversions ride RabbitMQ or Azure Service Bus, bringing enterprise message brokers into the stack for bulk energy data processing (exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:106).
- REST endpoints such as `POST /api/units/convert` and `/convert/batch` are documented alongside strict parity tests requiring the new C# service to match legacy C++ results exactly, reinforcing a modernization effort that cannot deviate from existing market calculations (exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:480, exemplars/technical-specifications/integration-points-exemplar.md:753).
